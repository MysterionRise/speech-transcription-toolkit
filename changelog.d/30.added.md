`Transcriber.transcribe()` and `transcribe()` take `progress=`, a function called with a `ProgressEvent` (`stage`, `done`, `total` and the newest `segment`) as decoding, transcription and speaker labelling advance; faster-whisper reports each segment as soon as it is decoded, and raising an exception in the function cancels the transcription. The library now logs to the `speech_toolkit` logger, which shows nothing until you configure logging, and `transcribe` takes `-v`/`--verbose` and `--log-level` to show its messages; `-q` now also hides Hugging Face download bars and transformers' progress bars and messages.
