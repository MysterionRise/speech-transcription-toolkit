`--batch-size N` runs faster-whisper's batched pipeline, several times faster on a GPU, and sets how many chunks Voxtral, Canary and Parakeet transcribe at once (Parakeet now batches two by default); `--beam-size N` sets the beam search of whisper and faster-whisper (1 decodes greedily, the fastest). New backend options against made-up text, for whisper and faster-whisper: `-O condition_on_previous_text=false`, `-O no_speech_threshold=X` and `-O hallucination_silence_threshold=SECONDS`, plus faster-whisper's `-O num_workers=N`; `python -m benchmarks.run` takes `-O` options too, so one run compares settings.
