Subtitles are valid in more cases: WebVTT escapes `&`, `<` and `>` in the text and in speaker names (so `-->` in the text no longer breaks a cue), and srt and vtt cues no longer overlap or have zero length. `--max-line-width` now counts the srt speaker label, counts Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters as two columns, and wraps Chinese and Japanese text without adding spaces. Diarized txt output no longer crashes on a segment without a speaker, and an unknown output extension such as `.tsv` now warns before writing plain text.
