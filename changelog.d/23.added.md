`Transcriber.transcribe()` and `transcribe()` also take audio in memory: the file's content as `bytes`, a binary file object such as an upload or `sys.stdin.buffer`, or the samples as a 1-D float32 NumPy array at 16 kHz, M4A files recorded by phones included. With speaker labels, each recording is now decoded once instead of twice, and `result.duration` is set whenever the audio was decoded and by the Parakeet, Canary and Voxtral backends; custom backends can declare the new `waveform` capability to get the decoded samples instead of a file.
