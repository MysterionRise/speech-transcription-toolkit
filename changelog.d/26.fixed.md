Speaker labels are cleaner: one word given to the wrong speaker no longer splits a line in three, a word in a pause goes to the nearer speaker, and merging speakers into a long transcript is much faster. In `speaker_segments`, each segment now has a unique `id` and a `segment_id`, the `id` of the transcript segment it comes from (use it to match the two); pieces of a split segment drop `tokens` and Whisper's other decoding details. Speaker hints that contradict each other, such as `--min-speakers 3 --max-speakers 2`, now fail before any model loads, and audio shorter than half a second isn't diarized.
