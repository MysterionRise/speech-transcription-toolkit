`-m` takes Hugging Face repo ids and local models (a CTranslate2 folder for faster-whisper, a `.pt` file for whisper), and `--device` takes `auto`, `cuda:N` and, except with faster-whisper, `mps` for Apple GPUs. `--compute-type` sets the precision, and `-O key=value` sets a backend's options, such as faster-whisper's `cpu_threads` or Canary's and Voxtral's `max_new_tokens` (`compute_type=` and `options=` in Python); `--list-backends` lists them.
