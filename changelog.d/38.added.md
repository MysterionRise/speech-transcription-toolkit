New output formats: `tsv` (start and end in milliseconds and the text, as openai-whisper writes it, plus a `speaker` column with speaker labels) and `rttm` (one line per speaker turn, the standard diarization format; it needs `--diarize`). `--timestamps` starts each txt line with its time (`[00:01:02] text`), and `--word-level` marks when each word starts in vtt cues (`<00:00:01.200>`) for karaoke-style highlighting. `--max-lines` and `--max-cue-duration` limit the lines and the length of subtitle cues, and `--speaker-names "Alice,Bob"` renames the speakers, in the order they first speak, in every format. `transcribe --from-json result.json -f srt` writes a saved result in other formats without loading a model. In Python, `render()` and `save()` take `max_lines`, `max_cue_duration`, `speaker_names`, `timestamps`, `word_level` and `file_id`.
