`--vad` (`vad=True`) works with every backend: Whisper transcribes only the stretches of speech, its timestamps moved
back to the recording's, and Voxtral, Parakeet and Canary cut their chunks at the edges of the speech, so silence no
longer reaches the model or fills with made-up text. `-O vad_threshold=`, `vad_min_silence=` and `vad_padding=` tune it,
faster-whisper's built-in filter too; in Python, `speech_toolkit.vad.detect_speech()` finds the speech and
`split_audio(..., regions=...)` cuts chunks from it.
