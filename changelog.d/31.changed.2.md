**Breaking:** `transcribe-server` now decodes each upload itself, once, and gives the model the samples, so every backend accepts the same formats: the common, self-contained audio and video formats, such as WAV, FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A, MP4, Ogg, Opus, WebM, MKV, WMA and AIFF. An upload in another format, which some backends decoded before, gets a 400: convert it first, for example to WAV with `ffmpeg -i input -ac 1 -ar 16000 output.wav`.
