ffmpeg, which decodes audio for the Parakeet, Canary and Voxtral backends and for speaker labels, now opens only local files, never URLs or other protocols, and runs without your environment's secrets such as `HF_TOKEN`. For untrusted input, `speech_toolkit.media.load_audio()` takes `strict=True`, which accepts only common audio and video formats and refuses playlists and concat lists, as well as a `timeout` and a `max_seconds` length cap.
